Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a Karvii customer.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash — never in plain text).
  • Order information: shipping address, phone number, and order details.
  • Payment information: processed entirely by Razorpay. Karvii never stores your card number or UPI PIN.
  • Usage data: pages visited, products viewed — used only to improve your shopping experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders.
  • To send order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • To improve our website, catalog, and services.
  • To respond to your support queries.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Cookies

We use a session cookie to keep you logged in and to remember your shopping cart. No tracking cookies from ad networks are used.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Razorpay — payment processing. Their privacy policy applies to payment data.
  • Google OAuth — optional login with Google. Governed by Google's privacy policy.

5. Data Security

Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms. We use HTTPS for all data in transit. We retain your data only as long as your account is active.

6. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us at vaibhavtiw2008@gmail.com.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Write to us at vaibhavtiw2008@gmail.com.
